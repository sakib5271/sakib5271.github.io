Refining the Ideal Customer Profile: A Propensity Model for Post-Trial Churn Reduction

Optimizely · Customer Success Analytics · 2023–2025

The prevailing ICP for Optimizely's enterprise A/B testing platform was built on heuristic firmographic criteria, failing to capture the behavioural and engagement signals that distinguish retained from churned accounts. This project rebuilt the ICP using a propensity-to-churn model trained on Mixpanel event data, Snowflake usage metrics, and support interaction history — producing a ranked segmentation that enabled targeted intervention by the global CS team across three regions.

// Key Findings
  • Refined ICP model reduced post-trial churn by ~13%, translating directly to measurable ARR retention across the SMB and mid-market segments.
  • Multi-product adoption rate improved from 1.6 to 2.2 products per account through whitespace analysis identifying underutilised feature adoption pathways.
  • Delivered $800K in expansion ARR in the SMB segment by operationalising an Experimentation Maturity Score to drive targeted upsell conversations.
  • Self-serve reporting platform reduced CS analytics turnaround from 3 days to same-day, enabling real-time playbook execution across EMEA, APJ, and NA.

Revenue Forecasting in Volatile Telecom Markets: An Ensemble Time-Series Approach

Robi Axiata Ltd. · Product & Pricing · 2022–2023

Revenue planning at Robi Axiata relied on spreadsheet extrapolations that systematically underperformed against structural breaks — regulatory changes, competitor pricing shocks, and seasonal OTT consumption patterns. This project designed and deployed an ensemble forecasting framework combining ARIMA, SARIMAX, and Exponential Smoothing models, with regime-detection logic to handle structural breaks in subscriber ARPU trends.

// Key Findings
  • Ensemble model improved forecasting accuracy by 17% vs. baseline spreadsheet projections, measured on MAPE across a 6-month holdout.
  • SARIMAX with exogenous competitor pricing variables outperformed univariate ARIMA by 9pp MAPE — confirming market-level signals improve within-firm forecasts.
  • Price elasticity model built in parallel identified optimal price points across 4 market segments, informing ARPU growth strategy for OTT bundle products.

Analytical Philosophy

How I Think About Data

"Seven years inside the organisations that data science writes about — building the systems executives rely on, and studying exactly where rigorous analysis breaks down in practice."

Across Robi Axiata, Optimizely, and Field Nation, I built analytical systems for pricing decisions, customer investment, and market strategy. I consistently observed the same pattern: organisations with excellent data and capable teams still made systematically suboptimal decisions — not because of modelling failures, but because of the gap between analytical output and organisational decision-making processes.

My practice is built around closing that gap. I focus on: predictive models that remain reliable when deployed in real decision contexts; AI adoption frameworks that work across industry and firm-maturity stages; and analytical systems — from ICP models to forecasting pipelines — designed to be simultaneously statistically credible and operationally trustworthy.

The most valuable analytical work is not the most technically sophisticated — it is the work that changes what an organisation actually does. Every project in this portfolio was built with that standard in mind.
